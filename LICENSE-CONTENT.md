## Authors' rights and opt-out

Posters and slides were uploaded by their presenters for display at the
workshop. They are published here under CC BY 4.0 by default. If a file carries
its own copyright or license statement, that statement takes precedence.

Authors who would like a different license, a correction, or their file
removed can ask via
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/smithara/hie-2026/issues), and we will update
the site.

## How to credit

Credit the authors as listed on the poster or slides, link to the source, and
name the license. For example:

> Lastname, F. et al. (2026). *Poster title*. Poster at ESLAB & Heliophysics in
> Europe #3, ESA/ESTEC. `https://2026.heliophysics.eu/posters/#<slug>`.
> CC BY 4.0

For talk slides:

> Lastname, F. (2026). *Talk title*. Talk at ESLAB & Heliophysics in Europe #3,
